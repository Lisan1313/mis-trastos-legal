Stuff Atlas privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Leer en español

In short: Stuff Atlas does not collect any of your data. Your photos and everything you add stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no servers of ours, and we never send your information anywhere.

What is stored and where

The app stores what you add to it only on your phone’s storage:

  • The photos you take, or pick from your gallery, for each thing and each place.
  • Your homes, your places (rooms, wardrobes, boxes…) and the things in each one.
  • Any details you choose to add to a thing: name, quantity, notes, category, value, serial number, barcode, and purchase, warranty or expiry dates.
  • Your preferences: which reminders are on and how far in advance.

The app never sends this information anywhere: not to us, not to anyone. We have no servers to receive it, so we have no access to it.

What we do NOT do

  • We don’t ask for your name, your email or any personal details.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign-up.
  • We don’t use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • We don’t share, sell or hand over data to third parties, because we don’t have any.
  • We don’t access your location.
  • Scanning a barcode doesn’t look anything up online: it’s only matched against the barcodes you have saved in the app.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of your things and to read QR labels and barcodes. It only turns on when you open those screens, and photos are kept inside the app.
  • Gallery photos: the app has no access to your gallery. When you pick photos, you do it with Android’s own picker, and the app only receives the ones you choose.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on warranty or expiry reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; they don’t come from any server.
  • Files: only when you tap to save or load a backup, export to Excel or save a PDF, through the system file picker.

Backups, PDFs and Excel files

The app lets you create a backup file with your data and photos, so you don’t lose them when you change phones. You can also create a PDF (labels or the insurance report) or a file for Excel. You create these files and you decide what to do with them: keep them on your phone, put them in your cloud, print them or send them to someone. The app never uploads them anywhere on its own. Once you save them to another service, that service’s terms apply.

Android’s automatic backup is turned off for this app: your data is not copied to any cloud by itself. That’s why it’s a good idea to make a backup yourself before changing phones.

In-app purchases

If you buy “Full Home”, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card details. Google only tells us whether the purchase has been made on your account, so you get it back if you reinstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, whatever their age.

How to delete your data

There are two ways, and both are immediate and permanent:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all data. All homes, places, things and photos are deleted.
  • Uninstalling the app: everything it stored on the phone is deleted.

There’s no need to ask us for anything, because we don’t keep any copy. Backups or PDFs you have saved outside the app are not deleted automatically: they are your own files.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changed the way it handles data, we would update this page and say so in the app itself before it happens.
